# Profile

Who your human is, for the assistant. Fill this in by hand or let the robot add to it as it learns. Keep it to what an assistant needs to serve them well.

## Who
- Name, what to call them, where they live, time zone.
- Family and close people.

## Work
- What they do, the projects that matter right now.

## Conventions
- Where notes and tasks live (an Obsidian vault? a folder in iCloud Drive?), naming habits, calendars and what goes on which.

## How they like it
- Tone, length, when to ask versus just do.
