# Shared memory

What your human asked an assistant to remember, and things worth keeping. One dated line per fact, added with Edit under the right heading (never rewrite the file). Correct a fact by editing its line; retire one by deleting it.

## People
Family, friends, clients, contractors, vendors: who they are and what matters about them.

## Preferences
How your human likes things done, said, scheduled, made.

## Projects and open loops
Decisions made, things promised, where a piece of work stands.

## Facts
Everything else worth keeping: numbers, dates, where things live.
